Biography

Hildairy is a digital artist that works by doing drawings and animations, to then post it in social media like Youtube, Instagram and TikTok. One of Hildairy’s achievements in her career is getting 800k views in a Youtube video about an animation of a very known indie game. One day Hildairy wants to create her own video game since video games have been a part of almost her whole life. And with this Hildairy hopes that her work can put a smile into your face or be inspired by it.
